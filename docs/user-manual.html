Introduction

Welcome to KSE

One app for your whole student life in Khulna.

KSE — the Khulna Student Ecosystem — brings every opportunity and daily need of student life together in one place. Instead of scrolling random Facebook groups and missing deadlines, you get a clean, verified, searchable app made for students.

1.1 What you can do with KSE

🎓 Discover opportunities
Internships, scholarships, events and workshops — manually curated and verified before publishing.
🏠 Find housing
Bachelor To-Let listings: rooms, sublets, host families and mess seats near campus.
📚 Get a tutor
Search verified tutors by subject, university, location and fee — or apply to become one.
🍜 Run your mess
Meals, bazar duty, shared expenses, balances and monthly settlement — built for real messes.
🛍️ Student Hub
Local services, shops and student deals around your campus, suggested by students.
🤝 Communities
Follow clubs and study groups, take part in discussions, polls and community events.
📖 Book Exchange
Sell, swap or give away used textbooks to students who need them next.
🔬 Research Partners
Publish your research profile and find a collaborator for your next project.

1.2 Who this manual is for

This manual covers the KSE mobile app end to end — every screen and every action, written in plain English. You don't need any technical knowledge. Read it from the start, or jump straight to the chapter you need from the contents page.

Quality promise: every internship, scholarship, event and tutor on KSE is reviewed by the KSE team before it becomes visible. Items marked “Manually curated” were checked by a human, not scraped by a bot.

1.3 A quick word on words

TermMeaning on KSE
BazarThe grocery shopping trip for your mess — who buys, what was bought, and what it cost.
SettlementThe end-of-month calculation that shows exactly who owes what in your mess.
To-LetA room, sublet or seat advertised for rent — listed by students, for students.
BookmarkedAn opportunity you saved for later — it appears on your Saved list and Dashboard.
ManagerThe member of a mess who approves joins, records expenses and locks the monthly settlement.

Setup

Getting Started

Install the app, create your account and finish the welcome tour.

2.1 Install KSE on your phone

  • Android: install the KSE.apk file (allow “Install from unknown sources” when asked), or get KSE from the Play Store when published.
  • iPhone: install through TestFlight or the App Store when published.
  • Web: open the KSE web app in any modern browser — your account works everywhere.

2.2 Create an account

  1. Open KSE. On the Login screen tap Create an account.
  2. Enter your Full name, Email and a Password (8+ characters).
  3. Tap Create account.
  4. If email confirmation is enabled for your region, check your inbox for the verification email first — then sign in.

Prefer Google?

On the Login screen tap Continue with Google and pick the account you want to use. No password to remember — the same profile is created for you automatically.

2.3 Sign in and reset your password

  • Signing in: enter your Email and Password and tap Sign in.
  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to you.

2.4 The welcome tour (onboarding)

The first time you sign in, KSE walks you through three short screens:

Tip: the richer your profile (university, department, skills), the better KSE can recommend opportunities for you. Chapter 13 shows you how.

4.2 Browsing and filtering a list

Open any category (say, Internships) and you get a card list: title, organization, location, mode and deadline. Buttons at the top of the list:

  • Filters — a sheet slides up with the filters available for that category.
  • Load more — appends the next page of results; lists are paginated to stay fast.

Typical filters

Mode: Remote / On-site / Hybrid Deadline: e.g. next 7 days Category or type Sort: most recent / rent high → low (To-Let)

Tap Apply to filter, or Reset to start over. Scholarship, To-Let and Tutor lists add their own filters (funding type, room type, subject, fee…).

4.3 The opportunity detail page

Tap any card to open the full detail. What you'll find:

📌 The facts
Title, organization, type, location, mode (remote / on-site / hybrid), eligibility and the full description.
🗓 Deadlines
Application deadline for internships & scholarships; Registration deadline for events & workshops.
🔖 Save it
The bookmark button adds the item to your Saved list so it lands on your Dashboard deadline tracker.
🚀 Apply / Register
Apply now opens the official application page in your browser. Events and workshops show Register instead.

Two states change the buttons: once the deadline passes you'll see Deadline passed (disabled), and events that closed show Registration closed. Items reviewed by the KSE team carry the Manually curated badge.

Why we send you to the official link: KSE shows you the opportunity and reminds you of the deadline — the application itself happens on the organizer's official page, so nothing gets lost in translation.

4.4 Events & workshops registration

  1. Open Explore → Events (or Workshops).
  2. Filter if you like — by date, organizer, location or online/offline.
  3. Tap an event and press Register.
  4. Your registrations appear on your Dashboard under registrations.

Funding your studies

Scholarships & the Application Tracker

Find funding, then manage every application from Saved to Selected.

Scholarships work like any opportunity list — the magic is the built-in application tracker that keeps your whole scholarship campaign organized.

5.1 Find scholarships

  1. Go to Explore → Scholarships.
  2. Use filters such as local / international, university, degree level and funding type to narrow the list.
  3. Open a scholarship to read the eligibility, funding details and deadline.
  4. Tap Apply now to open the official application page.

5.2 Track an application

On any scholarship detail page, tap Add to application tracker (or Update tracker if you already track it) and pick a status:

Saved Interested Preparing Applied Rejected Selected

Update the status any time as you progress — KSE confirms each change so you always know the tracker took it.

5.3 My Scholarships

Open Profile → My Scholarships to see every tracked scholarship in one list, each with a colored status chip and its deadline. It's your campaign overview — nothing gets forgotten the week it's due, because tracked items also feed your Dashboard deadlines.

Workflow suggestion: mark everything plausible as Interested while you browse, promote the real candidates to Preparing a few weeks out, and switch to Applied the moment you submit. Watching the list turn green is weirdly satisfying.

Student housing

Bachelor To-Let

Rooms, sublets, host families and mess seats — by students, for students.

“Bachelor To-Let” is KSE's housing market: verified listings posted by students and landlords, filtered for exactly what a student in Khulna needs.

6.1 Find a place

  1. Open Explore → Bachelor To-Let (or a Hot To-Let card on Home).
  2. Search by area, landmark or room type in the search bar.
  3. Tap Filters to narrow by city (Khulna, Jessore, Rajshahi…), area, room type and gender preference; sort by most recent or rent.
  4. Tap a listing for photos, rent, address details and the person to contact.

Room types you'll see

Single roomMess sublet Host family…and other student-friendly types

6.2 Post a listing

Got a room, sublet or mess seat to offer?

  1. From the To-Let list tap Post a listing (or use ＋ → Post a To-Let).
  2. Fill in the details: title, area, room type, rent, gender preference and description.
  3. Add photos (up to the per-listing photo limit — grant photos access when asked).
  4. Submit. Your listing first goes to Pending review — the KSE team checks it before it goes live.

6.3 Manage your listings

Tap My listings on the To-Let screen to see your posts and their status:

Pending review Published Rejected Expired Archived

No longer renting? Open the listing and tap Withdraw — after confirming, the listing is no longer visible to other students.

Safety first: visit the place with a friend before paying anything, and keep conversations inside your normal channels. KSE verifies listings, but you should always verify the person.

Teaching & learning

Tuition — Find a Tutor or Become One

Verified tutors near your university, and a tutor application for students who teach.

The Tuition area is a discovery + request workflow — simple on purpose. Find the right tutor, send a request, and track its status. No marketplace, no middleman fees.

7.1 Find a tutor

  1. Open Explore → Tuition.
  2. Filter by subject, university, location and expected monthly fee.
  3. Tutors carrying the ✓ Verified badge have been checked by the KSE team.
  4. Open a tutor to see their full profile: About, Subjects, Availability, University, Location, fee and Reviews from other students.

7.2 Send a tuition request

  1. On a tutor's profile tap Request tuition.
  2. Confirm the subject (and tutor), write a short message, and optionally add a preferred time.
  3. Tap Send request.

Track your requests

Open My tuition requests (from the Tuition area or your ＋ sheet). Each request shows:

Pending Accepted Rejected Closed

7.3 Become a tutor

If you teach (or want to), apply once — your tutor card then appears in discovery:

  1. Go to Profile → Become a Tutor.
  2. Pick the subjects you teach (at least one).
  3. Set your monthly fee range in taka (whole numbers, e.g. 2500) and your location.
  4. Write a short bio — e.g. “CSE undergrad teaching programming fundamentals” — and Submit application.

The KSE team reviews applications. Once approved you're listed as a tutor; if an application isn't approved you can simply apply again.

Tutor tip: a specific bio beats a general one. “CSE undergrad teaching C and Python to 1st-years” gets better requests than “I teach everything”.

Around campus

Student Hub

Local services, shops and student deals — built by students, for students.

The Student Hub answers everyday questions: where's the nearest laundromat, which shop gives a student discount, who repairs bicycles near campus. Content is people-powered — suggested by students and reviewed before going live.

8.1 Browse the Hub

  1. Open Explore → Student Hub (or the Quick Access tile).
  2. Category cards group the Hub — for example Study & Research, Daily Life, Mobility and Student Deals — each with a live listing count.
  3. Tap a category, then a listing, for details like address, phone, opening hours and what the place is known for.
  4. Use the Hub search to find a place by name or keyword directly.

8.2 Suggest a place

Know a shop or service other students would love? Add it in a minute:

  1. Tap Suggest a place (Hub screen or the ＋ quick actions).
  2. Fill in the place name (e.g. “New Star Laundry”), category and service type (e.g. “Wash, Dry cleaning, Pickup”).
  3. Add the city (e.g. Khulna), area (e.g. Shibbari), street address and a landmark.
  4. Add a phone number, opening hours (e.g. “Sat–Thu 9:00–21:00”) and a one-line summary of what the place is known for.
  5. Tap Send suggestion — the KSE team reviews it, then publishes it to the Hub.
Good suggestions are specific: exact landmarks, real opening hours and the one thing the place is genuinely known for make a listing useful for everyone.

Reuse · save money

Book Exchange

Swap, sell or give away the books you're done with.

Every semester ends with books you'll never open again — and every semester starts with someone hunting for exactly those books. Book Exchange connects the two.

9.1 Find a book

  1. Open Explore → Book Exchange.
  2. Search or browse listings — each shows the title, author, edition, subject, condition and asking price.
  3. Open a listing for the full description, condition notes and the seller's phone number.

9.2 Post a book

  1. Tap Post a book (or ＋ → Sell or exchange a book).
  2. Enter the book title, author, edition and subject.
  3. Choose the condition and set your price in BDT.
  4. Pick what the book is available for — sale, exchange or giveaway — and, for exchanges, which book you'd accept in return.
  5. Add condition notes (markings, meeting place…) and a phone number so buyers can reach you.
  6. Attach a cover photo, then submit for review.

9.3 Manage your listings

My listings on the Book Exchange screen shows everything you've posted and its review status — same lifecycle as To-Let: pending review → published.

Meet smart: arrange handovers on campus or another public place in daylight, and check the book before paying.

Collaborate

Research Partners

Publish your research profile and find your next collaborator.

Great projects rarely happen alone. Research Partners is a small, focused directory of students who want to collaborate on research — thesis work, competitions, papers.

10.1 Publish your research profile

  1. Open Explore → Research Partners and tap My research profile.
  2. Enter your research interest (e.g. “Computer Vision”) — required.
  3. Add your skills and your university / institution (e.g. KUET).
  4. Save — your profile now appears in the directory for other students to find.

10.2 Find a collaborator

  • Search by research interest, topic or skill — e.g. “machine learning” or “survey design”.
  • Browse the cards to see each student's interest, skills and institution.
  • Open a profile and use the contact details to start the conversation.
  • Load more appends further results.
Tip: keep your interest line specific — “Computer Vision — medical imaging” attracts the right partners far faster than “technology”.

KSE's mess module replaces the notebook on the fridge. Everyone sees meals and costs in real time, and the month ends with a settlement everyone can check line by line. Reach it from the Home Mess tile or ＋ quick actions.

11.1 Create or join a mess

➕ Create a Mess
Set the mess name (e.g. “12 No. Bachelor Mess”), location (e.g. Boyra, Khulna) and max members. You become the mess manager.
🔑 Join with Code
Enter the mess code (e.g. MESS-8F42) your manager shares, plus the invite code if the mess uses one. Your request waits for approval.

The Mess Hub lists every mess you belong to — active messes open with a tap, and pending ones show “Waiting for manager approval”. One person can be in several messes.

11.2 Mess Home — today in seconds

Mess Home shows:

  • Today's meals — breakfast, lunch, dinner, each with a one-tap ON/OFF toggle. Meal cut-off times (set by the manager) close each window — after that KSE tells you exactly when it closed.
  • Your balance for the month and the current meal rate.
  • Your next bazar duty — with a button to request an exchange from the member's own screen.
  • Announcements from the manager, as one subtle banner.
  • Incoming exchange requests for your duties — accept or reject them right there.

11.3 Meals

The Meals tab manages today plus the whole month:

  • Toggle today's meals on/off (respecting cut-offs).
  • A monthly calendar shows every member's meals per day — check any past or future date.
  • Switch months to review history before settlement.

11.4 Bazar — entries and duty

Record a bazar trip

  1. Open the Bazar tab and tap Add Bazar.
  2. For each item, tap Add Item and enter the name, quantity, unit and price.
  3. Tag items by category: Vegetables Grocery Spices Snacks Cleaning Other.
  4. Tap Save Bazar — the month total updates instantly and every member sees the entry.

The duty calendar

  • Bazar → Duty shows who buys on which day — your own duties are highlighted so “when do I have to buy?” is answered at a glance.
  • Managers assign, reassign and remove duties (Assign Duty).
  • Members request a duty exchange; the other member accepts or rejects, and accepted swaps update the calendar automatically.

11.5 Expenses

Shared fixed costs — rent, utilities, wifi — live in More → Expenses:

  • Everyone sees the month's expense total and transaction list.
  • The manager adds expenses (Add Expense — amount, date, description) and can delete mistakes.
  • Expenses feed the month's balance alongside bazar and meals.

11.6 Finance — your balance and payments

  • My Balance breaks the number down: meals eaten, bazar you paid for, shared expenses — every line visible.
  • Record a payment you made with Record payment (amount + date). Payments are auto-confirmed; the manager can also record or verify them later.
  • Payment statuses: Pending Confirmed Rejected.
  • Your full payment history stays in the Payments screen (also on the More tab).

11.7 Settlement — closing the month

Settlement turns the month into a final, checkable number. The manager drives it in three steps:

1 · Generate
Generate Settlement builds a draft from the month's meals, bazar and expenses — with the full breakdown shown.
2 · Publish
Publish shares the settlement with every member, each seeing their own final balance.
3 · Lock
Lock Month freezes the month for good — no more edits to meals, bazar or expenses.

Every member can open More → Settlement and inspect exactly how their number was calculated — no more notebook arithmetic arguments.

11.8 Members & manager tools

More → Members shows who lives in the mess. As manager you can:

  • Accept / reject join requests from people who used your mess code.
  • Remove a member who moved out.
  • Make Manager — hand the manager role to another member when you leave.
  • See and share the mess invite code.

11.9 Mess settings

  • Mess information — name, location; members see it on the mess home.
  • Meal cut-offs — breakfast, lunch and dinner cut-off times (HH:MM). Defaults apply until you change them; after a cut-off, meal toggles for that meal close for everyone.
  • Announcements — post a notice that appears as the banner on every member's mess home.

Settings are manager-only; members get a read-only view so the rules are never a mystery.

Clubs & groups

Communities

Follow clubs, join discussions, vote in polls and attend community events.

The Community tab gathers student clubs, study groups and interest circles around Khulna — deliberately simple: posts, polls and events, not a full social network.

12.1 Find and follow communities

  • The tab opens with Recommended for you and Trending discussions across KSE.
  • Use Search communities… to find a club by name.
  • Open a community to see its description, university and members; tap Join community (then Joined to leave).

12.2 Posts & discussions

Inside a community the feed shows posts from members. As a member you can create four kinds:

Discussion Question Opportunity Announcement

  1. Tap the post composer (or ＋ in the community).
  2. Choose the type — e.g. Question asks “What do you want to ask?”; Opportunity asks you to share the opportunity, eligibility and link.
  3. Write your text and optionally add an image.
  4. Post — it appears in the feed immediately. Community moderators can pin posts as announcements.

Open any post to comment. “Be the first to comment” means exactly that — break the ice. Some older discussions are locked and read-only.

12.3 Polls

  1. In a community tap Create poll.
  2. Ask a clear question (at least 5 characters) and add the options.
  3. Choose when results are visible: Always visible After voting After poll closes.

12.4 Community events

  1. In a community tap Create event.
  2. Set the title, start and end time (format YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM), and the mode: Online On campus Hybrid.
  3. Publish — the event appears in the community's Community events section.

12.5 Request a new community

Want a community that doesn't exist yet? Tap Create in community on the Community tab, fill in the details (with an optional image) and submit. Your request goes to the KSE team for review — you'll see it marked “pending admin review” until it's approved.

Reporting: see something wrong in a community? Use the report (flag) action on the post — moderators review reports.

You, on KSE

Your Profile & Portfolio

Personal info, education, projects, certificates, achievements, research, resume and links.

Your profile powers better recommendations; your portfolio is the living CV you'll actually reuse for internship and scholarship applications.

13.1 The Profile tab

  • Your name and avatar, with a profile completion %.
  • Improve Profile / Edit profile — update your full name, university, department, academic level, phone, bio, interests and skills.
  • My portfolio — the full portfolio (below).
  • My Scholarships — your application tracker (Chapter 5).
  • Become a Tutor — tutor application (Chapter 7).
  • Quick links — Dashboard and Saved.
  • Settings and Sign out.
Why complete your profile? KSE recommends opportunities using your university, department, skills and interests — an empty profile gets generic results, a full one gets yours.

13.2 Portfolio sections

Open Profile → My portfolio. Every section supports add, edit and delete:

SectionWhat to add
🎓 EducationEducation level, institution (pick from the list — or request a missing one), district (Khulna division and beyond), details and dates.
📁 ProjectsTitle, description, tech stack, project link, showcase fields and dates.
🛡️ CertificatesTitle, issuer, issue date and certificate details.
🏆 AchievementsTitle, description and date — competitions, awards, honors.

13.3 Privacy

Portfolio and profile details are yours: public fields are clearly defined, and private items (resume, certificates) stay private to you. KSE asks for the minimum data a student platform needs — and never shares your phone number or email with other students.

Dashboard, Saved & Search

Deadlines at a glance, everything you bookmarked, and one search box for the whole app.

14.1 Dashboard

Open it from the ▦ grid on Home, the ＋ sheet, or Profile quick links:

Profile score gauge

Opportunity overview
A 2×2 grid of live published counts across opportunity types.

14.2 Saved (bookmarks)

  • Tap the bookmark icon on any opportunity card or detail to save it.
  • Open Profile → Saved to see everything, newest first.
  • Tap the bookmark again (card or detail) to remove it.

14.3 Search — one box for everything

Type in the search bar on Home or Explore and press enter to open the global search screen. It covers opportunities, tutors, communities and hub listings:

  1. Results update as you type — search titles, organizations and keywords.
  2. Narrow with the same filters you know from the lists (type, mode, deadline).
  3. Tap Clear search / Clear all to start over, or Load more for more results.
  4. Browse by category instead via the Explore categories row on the search screen.

If nothing matches, KSE suggests trying a different keyword or loosening the filters — and sometimes content just hasn't been published yet (“Verified listings are on their way”).

Why does a listing say “Pending review”?User-submitted content (To-Lets, books, places, communities) is checked by the KSE team before it becomes public.
An opportunity says “Deadline passed” — can I still apply?No. Once the deadline passes the button is disabled; use filters like “next 7 days” to find live ones.
Can I be in more than one mess?Yes — the Mess Hub lists all your messes; pending ones wait for manager approval.
Can I stop being a tutor?Contact the KSE team and they'll remove your tutor listing.
Does KSE work offline?Browsing needs internet. The app is built to be fast on normal mobile networks.
How do I delete my account?Request account deletion through the KSE support contact — your data is removed with it.
